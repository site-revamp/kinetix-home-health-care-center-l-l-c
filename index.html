Questions before your first appointment?

Ask the Kinetix team →
Where does Kinetix provide home visits?

Kinetix advertises home healthcare visits across Dubai, including residential properties, offices and hotels. Contact the team to confirm availability for your area.

Do I need a doctor’s referral to enquire?

Kinetix’s website states that a referral is not required to book a home physiotherapy consultation. If you have a referral or insurance requirements, keep the relevant information available when you enquire.

Are services available for children?

Yes. Kinetix lists paediatric physiotherapy and related therapy services. The appropriate care pathway depends on the child’s individual needs and clinical assessment.

How can I arrange an appointment?

You can call +971 58 999 6434, send a WhatsApp message or use the enquiry form on this page. The team can discuss your requirements and appointment availability.

Are the physiotherapists DHA-licensed?

Kinetix states that its physiotherapists are licensed by the Dubai Health Authority. You can contact the provider if you need further information about a clinician’s credentials.
